Annex 2: setups we haven't tested yet

Ad setups and goals each platform offers that we haven't run, sorted by what they serve: account growth (new followers and subscribers) or retention (the same people seeing Juniper again). From a quick read of each platform's documentation on Nov 24. The docs say how a setup works, not how well it does for an account like ours, so each one needs a small test.

Strongest candidates
  • A subscriber goal on YouTube. YouTube Promote ("audience growth") and Demand Gen ("YouTube engagements") both optimise for subscriptions. It is the one gap in our data: views have bought 0 subscribers so far. A small test answers it.
  • Lookalikes from our own pools. All three platforms can find new people who resemble the people who already engaged. Ready on YouTube and Instagram now; TikTok once its per-ad pools fill.
  • A video ad sequence on YouTube for Stage 2. Shows the same person a set series in order (for example stream clip, then animation, then Real Soon). It is repetition by design, which is what Stage 2 is for.
